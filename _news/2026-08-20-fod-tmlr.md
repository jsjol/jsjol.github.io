New paper in TMLR: [_Efficient Image Restoration with State-Dependent Forward Diffusion_](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Eq9k6Va3hY). Image restoration through a single forward diffusion process instead of the usual coupled forward-backward scheme, which keeps the model analytically tractable and allows few-step sampling. Work led by Ziwei Luo.
