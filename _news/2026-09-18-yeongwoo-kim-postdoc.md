[Yeongwoo Kim](https://www.uu.se/en/contact-and-organisation/staff?query=N26-2195) has joined the group as a postdoctoral researcher, co-supervised with [Leiting Zhang](https://www.uu.se/en/contact-and-organisation/staff?query=N21-45) at the Department of Chemistry and funded by [Compel](https://www.uu.se/en/disciplinary-domain/science-and-technology/research/research-collaborations/compel). The project develops switching dynamical systems that identify battery degradation mechanisms, such as particle fracture and gas evolution, as discrete modes in acoustic emission and electrochemical measurements.
