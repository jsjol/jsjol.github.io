Two papers accepted at [NeurIPS 2026](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026). In [_Learning inexact alternating minimization for convex problems with quadratic coupling_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12544), led by Paul Häusner, neural networks trained to approximate the subproblem solutions replace the iterative inner solvers of alternating minimization, which makes CT reconstruction up to 30 times faster at matched accuracy. In [_ESS-Flow: Training-free guidance of flow-based models as inference in source space_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05849), led by Adhithyan Kalaivanan, pretrained flow-based models are guided without gradients by sampling in their source space.
