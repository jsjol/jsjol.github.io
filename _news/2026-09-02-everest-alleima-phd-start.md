[Hampus Arnestrand](https://www.uu.se/en/contact-and-organisation/staff?query=N24-2518) has joined the group as a PhD student, co-supervised with Ida-Maria Sintorn. He works in Everest, a Vinnova-funded project with Alleima and Sandvik that aims to bring down the capital cost of electrolyzers for green hydrogen. Our part is Bayesian experiment design for porous electrodes whose microstructure can only be observed through X-ray tomography.
